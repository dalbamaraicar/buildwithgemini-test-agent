# Project Brief: Smart Pantry Chef

## 🎯 Domain & One-liner
A smart recipe & pantry concierge that manages pantry inventory in Firestore, recommends recipes based on available ingredients, and updates item quantities.

## 🛠️ Tool Coverage
1. **Memory**: Remembers user dietary preferences, restrictions, and cooking goals.
2. **Tools**: Reads and updates items in the `pantry` Firestore collection.
3. **Catalog & A2UI**: Displays pantry inventory cards and recipe recommendations.
4. **Visuals**: Generates dish concept images.
5. **Compute**: Calculates scaled ingredient portions and nutrition macros.
